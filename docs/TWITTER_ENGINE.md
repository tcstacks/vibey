# Twitter/X Growth Engine Guide

The Twitter/X Growth Engine helps you build an audience and accelerate idea validation through strategic Twitter presence.

## Overview

Building in public on Twitter accelerates validation by:
- Attracting early adopters who want to follow your journey
- Getting real-time feedback on your ideas
- Building trust before you have a product
- Creating distribution for your landing pages

## Getting Started

### 1. Account Setup

Navigate to **Twitter** in the sidebar to access the Twitter module.

Your account dashboard shows:
- Current follower count and growth
- Daily limits (tweets, follows, DMs, likes)
- Account health status
- Engagement rate

**Account Stages**:
- `0-1k`: Focus on engagement and relationship building
- `1k-10k`: Balance content creation with engagement
- `10k+`: Optimize for reach and conversions

### 2. Health Monitoring

Keep your account healthy to maintain reach:

| Status | Meaning | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Healthy | No issues | Continue normal activity |
| Warning | Approaching limits | Reduce activity |
| Restricted | Action blocked | Wait 24-48 hours |

**Avoid restrictions by**:
- Staying under daily limits
- Not following/unfollowing rapidly
- Engaging authentically (not spamming)

## Relationship CRM

The CRM helps you build strategic relationships systematically.

### Relationship Tiers

**Whale (10k+ followers)**
- High-influence accounts in your niche
- Engage thoughtfully on their content
- Goal: Get noticed, build relationship over time
- Expected reciprocity: Low initially

**Peer (Similar stage)**
- Fellow builders at your stage
- Mutual support and collaboration
- Goal: Build genuine friendships
- Expected reciprocity: High

**Fan (Your engaged followers)**
- People who consistently engage with you
- Nurture and appreciate them
- Goal: Build community
- Expected reciprocity: Very high

### Adding Relationships

1. Go to **Twitter > Relationships**
2. Click **"Add Relationship"**
3. Fill in:
   - Twitter handle
   - Display name
   - Tier (whale/peer/fan)
   - Niche tags
   - Follow status (mutual or one-way)
   - Priority (high/medium/low)
   - Notes

### Tracking Interactions

Each relationship tracks:
- **Total replies**: Your replies to their tweets
- **Total likes**: Likes on their content
- **Total DMs**: Direct message exchanges
- **Reciprocity score**: How much they engage back
- **Last interaction**: When you last engaged

View interaction history on the relationship detail page.

## Content Studio

Create and manage your Twitter content.

### Tweet Types

**Single Tweets**
- Standalone thoughts
- Best for engagement and replies
- 280 character limit

**Threads**
- Multi-tweet narratives
- Best for teaching and storytelling
- Higher impressions potential

**Replies**
- Responses to others' tweets
- Best for relationship building
- Can drive profile visits

### Composing Content

1. Go to **Twitter > Compose**
2. Select tweet type
3. Write your content
4. Optionally link to an idea (for tracking)
5. Save as draft or schedule

### Content Derived from Research

Link tweets to your validation research:
- Pain points become relatable content
- Interview quotes become testimonials
- Competitor gaps become positioning

Select "Derived from" when composing to track this.

### Thread Structures

Use proven structures for threads:
- **Listicle**: "7 ways to..."
- **Story**: Personal narrative with lesson
- **How-to**: Step-by-step guide
- **Contrarian**: Challenge common belief
- **Case Study**: Real results breakdown

## Engagement Queue

Systematic engagement builds relationships faster.

### How It Works

1. System identifies engagement opportunities
2. Targets are queued by priority
3. You work through the queue daily
4. Interactions are logged automatically

### Working the Queue

1. Go to **Twitter > Engagement**
2. View pending engagements
3. For each target:
   - Read the tweet
   - Use or modify the suggested reply
   - Click "Mark Complete" after replying
   - Or "Skip" if not relevant

### Priority System

| Priority | Target | Action |
|----------|--------|--------|
| Critical | Whale mentioned you | Respond immediately |
| High | Whale's new post | Engage within hours |
| Medium | Peer's content | Engage same day |
| Low | General network | When time permits |

### Tips for Great Replies

- Add genuine value (insight, experience, question)
- Don't just say "Great thread!"
- Reference specific points they made
- Share relevant personal experience
- Ask thoughtful questions

## Build-in-Public Automation

Automatically celebrate milestones to share your journey.

### Milestone Types

- **MRR**: Revenue milestones ($100, $1k, $10k)
- **Users**: User count milestones (10, 100, 1000)
- **Followers**: Follower milestones (100, 500, 1k)
- **Launch**: Product launches
- **Custom**: Any milestone you define

### Setting Up Milestones

1. Go to **Twitter > Milestones** (from dashboard)
2. Click **"Add Milestone"**
3. Select type and threshold
4. Write or use template content
5. System tracks progress automatically

### Workflow

1. Milestone threshold is reached
2. Content is auto-generated from template
3. You review and approve
4. Tweet is scheduled or published
5. Engagement is tracked

## Analytics

Track your Twitter growth and optimize.

### Key Metrics

**Growth**
- Follower velocity (new followers per day)
- Net gain (followers minus unfollows)
- Projected monthly growth

**Content Performance**
- Average impressions per tweet
- Average engagement rate
- Top performing content
- Worst performing content

**Engagement ROI**
- Profile visits from replies
- Followers gained from engagement
- Reply-to-follow ratio

**Conversion**
- Landing page clicks from Twitter
- Waitlist signups attributed to Twitter
- Conversion rate

### Optimization Insights

The analytics surface:
- Best posting times
- Best content types
- Top performing hashtags
- Most valuable relationships

## Best Practices

### Daily Routine (30 min)

1. **5 min**: Check account health
2. **10 min**: Work engagement queue
3. **10 min**: Post or schedule content
4. **5 min**: Log new relationships

### Weekly Review

- Review growth metrics
- Analyze top performing content
- Update relationship priorities
- Plan next week's content

### Content Calendar

| Day | Content Type |
|-----|--------------|
| Mon | Build-in-public update |
| Tue | Thread (educational) |
| Wed | Engage only (no posts) |
| Thu | Contrarian take |
| Fri | Personal story |
| Sat | Curated insights |
| Sun | Week reflection |

### Engagement Ratios

For sustainable growth:
- **Replies to others**: 60% of activity
- **Original content**: 30% of activity
- **Retweets/shares**: 10% of activity

## Linking to Validation

Connect Twitter activity to your ideas:

1. When composing, select the related idea
2. Track which tweets drive landing page visits
3. Attribute waitlist signups to specific content
4. Measure Twitter's contribution to validation

This creates a feedback loop:
Research -> Content -> Traffic -> Signups -> More Research
